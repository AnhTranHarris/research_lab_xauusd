Core equations used across the library
For trade i with entry price P_i^0, signed direction d_i ∈ {+1,-1}, and observed mark P_i(t):
Signed excursion:
E_i(t) = d_i [P_i(t) - P_i^0]
Maximum favorable excursion:
MFE_i(t) = max_{0≤u≤t} E_i(u)
Maximum adverse excursion:
MAE_i(t) = max_{0≤u≤t} [-E_i(u)]
Net P&L before account-level sizing:
π_i = V_i · d_i [P_i^exit - P_i^0] - C_i
where V_i is the monetary value per price unit for the executed volume and C_i contains spread, commission, slippage, and modeled execution costs.
Profit factor:
PF = Σ_i max(π_i,0) / |Σ_i min(π_i,0)|
Win rate:
WR = N(π_i>0) / N
Net expectancy:
E[π] = (1/N) Σ_i π_i = WR·E[π|π>0] - (1-WR)·E[-π|π<0]
Gross-loss efficiency objective:
J = NetProfit - λ·|GrossLoss| - μ·MaxDrawdown
where λ and μ are research penalties used to avoid optimizing net profit while tolerating destructive loss geometry.
Action-value routing:
a_t ∈ {CONTINUE, FADE, ABSTAIN}
a_t* = argmax_a E[R_{t:t+H}(a) | x_t]
where x_t contains only completed/causal state information at t.
